Dates take a time of day in the app, the web UI, Emacs and samu bulk date. The web UI copies and exports tasks as IDs, Markdown, Org, TSV or JSON: Ctrl+A picks every row, and the settings save a list or the whole account; samu list writes Markdown and Org. A block of a perspective can carry its own search, and the web sidebar can be arranged and its entries hidden. A repetition without a date gets its first one, and a group found by a search brings its children.
